Intraday Trading Rules & Best Practices

Leverage & Margin Rules

  • MIS trades use 4-5x leverage: ₹20k margin = ₹80-100k buying power
  • Margin Call Trigger: Position loss ≥ 25-30% of margin → automatic warning
  • Forced Square-Off: 3:25 PM IST → all open MIS trades auto-closed at market price

Cost Advantage vs Delivery

  • STT: 0.025% (sell only) vs 0.1% (delivery) = 75% savings
  • Stamp Duty: 0.003% vs 0.015% = 80% savings
  • No DP charges (no settlement needed)
  • Total charges ~71% lower than delivery trades
  • Breakeven move: 0.084% vs 0.288% on delivery (3.4x easier!)

Risk Management

  • Only trade high-conviction setups (65%+ confidence required)
  • Stop-Loss: 1.5% of margin (hard limit, auto-closes)
  • Target: 2-3% profit per trade (realistic with leverage)
  • Max 2 concurrent positions during market hours
  • Never add to losing positions (no averaging down)

Timing

  • Trading Hours: 09:15 - 15:25 IST
  • Best Entry: 09:20-10:30 and 14:00-14:30 (high volatility + volume)
  • Avoid: 15:20-15:25 (forced square-off risk)
  • Always close by 15:20 to avoid forced liquidation slippage
